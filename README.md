## The joke

> My wife asked for dinner with a little thyme, so I made her wait an hour.

1 herb requested, 1 hour served late, 0 thyme in the pan: she
asked for the herb, he heard the hour, and the dinner paid
for the pun. A dinner-table homophone, delivered straight-faced.
